# Product steering

DME Direct is a BetterRX + DME+ provider workflow product for ordering and managing durable medical equipment from intake through delivery, acknowledgement, recovery, pickup, and executive reporting.

Prioritize clarity, safe progression, explainable decisions, and mobile usability. Keep the left-side action rail and the current low-noise visual language. Surface exceptions at the moment they can be resolved. Do not turn the product into a dense generic analytics dashboard.

Use synthetic data in development. Never introduce real patient, clinician, vendor-contact, financial, credential, or location data into the repository.
